Specialities & services

Cardiac Treatments & Procedures

Explore the cardiac procedures and interventions listed among Dr. Aditi Rastogi’s areas of expertise.

Coronary Angiography & Angioplasty

Procedures used to assess coronary arteries and treat certain narrowed or blocked arteries.

Complex Coronary PCI

Percutaneous coronary intervention for selected complex coronary artery conditions.

Pacemaker Implantation

Device implantation to help manage selected heart rhythm problems.

CRT – Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy

Device-based therapy for selected patients with specific heart-failure and electrical conduction conditions.

ICD – Implantable Cardioverter Defibrillator

Implantable device therapy for selected patients at risk of dangerous heart rhythms.

Balloon Valvotomy

A catheter-based procedure used in selected cases of narrowed heart valves.

Structural Heart Interventions – Device Closure

Selected minimally invasive procedures to close certain structural openings in the heart.

Peripheral Angioplasty

A catheter-based treatment for selected narrowed arteries outside the heart.
